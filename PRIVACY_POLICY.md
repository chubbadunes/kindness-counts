# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2025

This privacy policy applies to the mobile game listed on Google Play (package `com.chubbadunes.kindnessquest`), published by Chubba Dunes.

## We do not collect any personal information

The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no names, no email addresses, no photos, and no free-text input. Children interact only by tapping pre-written activities.

## What stays on your device

Game progress (completed kind missions, mood check-ins, streaks, garden) and the record that a purchase was made are stored only in the app's private storage on the device. A random, anonymous identifier is generated on first launch and is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of this data.

## Purchases

In-app purchases are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled by Google under Google's own privacy policy; the app never sees or stores card numbers or payment details.

## Advertising and third parties

The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics, ad networks, or social SDKs. No data is shared with third parties.

## Children

The app is designed for children and complies with COPPA and GDPR-K principles: no personal data is collected from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL and the "last updated" date above will change.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: chubbadunes (GitHub) or the developer email listed on the app's Google Play store listing.
